# Aflatoxin Testing Workshop

We are excited to announce our upcoming **Aflatoxin Testing Workshop**, a practical and insightful session focused on understanding how aflatoxins are detected and monitored to ensure food safety and protect public health. This workshop will introduce key laboratory concepts, testing procedures, and the importance of mycotoxin analysis in agriculture and food systems.

We especially encourage Year One students to attend, as this is a perfect opportunity to gain early exposure, build strong foundational knowledge, and confidently step into the world of laboratory science and research. Don’t miss this chance to start your journey with hands-on learning and meaningful engagement!
